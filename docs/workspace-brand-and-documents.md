# Workspace brand and document template

2 November 2026. Visual sources: the supplied Lutera design-system archive,
master logo files and brand book (19 October), plus the AIO procurement
specification supplied as a document-layout reference. The reference's project
codes, quantities, supplier requirements and confidential wording are not
company-wide business rules and are not copied into customer documents.

The workspace now imports the supplied design tokens. Lutera blue is **#2E5BFF**,
Navy **#161A3D**, Paper **#F3F2EE** and Signal cyan **#2BC4E8**. The sidebar uses
the exact Lutera blue. Sora 400/500/600, white cards, flat blue-wash panels,
rounded action buttons and compact checkboxes follow the supplied system.
Cyan is reserved for Navy surfaces. The approved logo files are placed intact.

The shared financial template covers quotations, pro formas, sales invoices,
receipts, sales orders, credit notes, purchase orders, supplier RFQs and delivery
notes. It uses the formal descriptor logo, Navy title panel, subtle Lab rows,
company/recipient columns, ruled tables, blue-wash summaries, wrapped text,
20 mm PDF margins and numbered continuation pages. RFQs and delivery notes show
descriptions and quantities without presenting an amount payable. Actual prices,
terms, identities and statuses continue to come from each authorised record.

PDFs embed Sora and JetBrains Mono (references/figures), with a bundled fallback
for symbols. Bank/tax/legal details sit in the 7.5 pt footer; unprovided records
are omitted. PNG text is outlined from
the packaged fonts, so it does not depend on fonts installed on Vercel. Editable
DOCX files name Sora and use the same palette and table hierarchy; a recipient's
Word installation may substitute fonts if Sora or JetBrains Mono is unavailable. Long PDF rows
continue over pages. Customer statements and electronic-approval pages use the
same brand palette. Staff business cards export at 85 × 55 mm, Navy branded front and Paper details
back, with 5 mm safe margins and optional 3 mm PDF bleed/crop marks. Portrait ID
cards use Navy fronts/backs, the approved cropped symbol, a Paper photo panel
and mono staff numbers at 54 × 86 mm.

Existing financial arithmetic, approvals, source snapshots, access checks and
manual-payment evidence requirements remain in force. Current authorised exports,
email attachments and portal downloads use the shared template; previously saved
files are not overwritten. No tax settings or opening balances are changed by
this visual update.

Validation includes strict types/build, all nine document types in PDF/DOCX/PNG,
long multipage tables, signed/current-version exports, card dimensions, responsive
workspace checks, accessibility checks, exact sidebar colour and checkbox sizing.
All generated review files use isolated fixtures, not real orders or customers.
